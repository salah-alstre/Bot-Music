PROMPT
------
Square app icon for a premium Discord music bot called "Sonaris". A minimal, modern, futuristic mark: a thin
glowing circular sonar ring (gradient from electric violet #7C5CFF to cyan #22D3EE) enclosing five vertical rounded
equalizer bars in a symmetrical waveform (short, medium, tall, medium, short) with a vertical violet-to-cyan gradient.
Deep near-black background (#0B0B12) with a soft violet radial glow behind the mark and two extremely faint concentric
"sonar ping" rings fading outward. Flat vector style with subtle light bloom, crisp edges, perfectly centered, strong
silhouette that stays recognizable at 32 px, the mark occupying the central 65-70 percent of the canvas so a circular
crop never cuts it. No text, no letters, no watermark, no photo-realism, no clutter, no gradients on the background
other than the soft glow. Elegant, calm, high-end tech aesthetic.

background, low contrast, blurry, jpeg artifacts, extra rings, off-center

- Safe area: keep all important shapes inside the central 700 x 700 px
- Palette: #0B0B12 (background), #7C5CFF (violet), #22D3EE (cyan)
